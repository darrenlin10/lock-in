Welcome to Lock-In

Every time you unlock your phone, see the one deadline that matters, how many days are left, and your next three steps.

  1. Pick a deadline and when it's due.
  2. Plan the steps, with Gemini or any AI assistant if you like.
  3. Save the wallpaper and set it as your lock screen, then add reminders to your calendar.

Free, no account. Everything stays on this device.

Choose one deadline

Pick a single deadline that matters. Lock-In keeps its exact date on the lock screen and counts remaining calendar days in the timezone you choose.

Deadline type
How the countdown is calculated
  • Date-only: remaining calendar days in the chosen timezone. Shows “Due today” on the date and “Overdue” after it.
  • Specific time: stores the exact instant. Before it, shows calendar days left (or “Due today”). When the instant passes, shows “Overdue”.
  • Days are counted by calendar date, so daylight-saving changes never shift the count.
  • A wallpaper is a snapshot, not a running timer. Every image carries an “As of” date and the exact due date.

    How to set it

    iPhone
    1. Tap Share / Save to Photos (or download, then save the image to Photos).
    2. Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photos, and pick the image. Or long-press the lock screen and tap “+”.
    3. Keep the image unzoomed so the paper stays where you placed it. Choose “Set as Wallpaper Pair” or customise the lock screen only.
    4. Check that clock style, widgets and notifications don't overlap the paper. If they do, adjust the paper position in step 3 and export again.
    Android
    1. Download the PNG or tap Share / Save to Photos.
    2. Open it in Photos/Gallery → ⋮ → Use as / Set as wallpaper → Lock screen. Labels vary by manufacturer.
    3. Check the clock style and any lock-screen widgets against the paper.
    Mac
    1. Choose a computer preset in step 3 that matches your display resolution.
    2. System Settings → Wallpaper → Add Photo… and select the PNG. On recent macOS versions the lock screen uses the desktop wallpaper.
    Windows
    1. Settings → Personalization → Lock screen → Personalize your lock screen → Picture → Browse photos.
    2. The Windows clock sits at the lower left; keep the paper clear of it.

          Settings

          Your data

          Everything is stored in this browser only, on this device. Back it up before clearing your browser data or switching devices. Backups never include an API key.

          How it works

          1. Deadline. Pick one deadline and when it's due. The countdown is worked out in the timezone you choose.
          2. Plan. Copy the planning prompt into Gemini or any AI assistant, check the plan it suggests, then paste it back. Or just type your steps.
          3. Design. Choose your phone or computer, a desk and a paper style. The preview warns you if anything is hard to read or hidden by your clock.
          4. Export. Save the image and set it as your lock screen, then tell Lock-In it's done. It will tell you when the wallpaper goes out of date (a new day, or a changed plan).
          5. Reminders. Add your steps to your calendar so you're reminded on the day.

          A wallpaper is a picture, so it doesn't change by itself. Open Lock-In and save a fresh one whenever your plan changes, or weekly (there's an optional reminder for that).

          Privacy

          • Lock-In has no server and no account. It doesn't collect, track or sell anything, and there are no ads or analytics.
          • Your deadline, plan, reminders and images are stored only in this browser. Clearing your browser data removes them, so keep a backup.
          • Information leaves this device only when you choose:
            • AI image generation (optional): only the style description is sent to the provider you pick, with your own key. Never your tasks or deadline. The key is kept in memory and forgotten when you close the page.
            • “Add to Google Calendar” links: open Google Calendar with that reminder's title and time filled in.
            • Copying the planning prompt: you paste it into the assistant of your choice yourself.
